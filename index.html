Korea Pack 🇰🇷

The on-the-ground cheat sheet — save this to your phone before Seoul.

K-ETA waived ✅ ₩ cash + card No tipping
💸

Money

Currency is the Korean won (₩). Rough math: ₩1,000 ≈ A$0.95, so ₩10,000 ≈ A$9–10.

  • Card works almost everywhere; carry some cash for markets, taxis and older spots.
  • Grab a T-money card at any convenience store — taps subway, bus and a lot of shops.
  • Use ATMs marked "Global" (airport, 7-Eleven, GS25) for foreign cards.
💸 Split expenses on Tricount ↗
📱

Data & apps

Grab an eSIM/SIM at the airport or pre-order — cheap unlimited data.

  • ⚠️ Google Maps barely does directions in Korea — use Naver Map or KakaoMap.
  • Papago for translation, KakaoTalk to message, Kakao T for taxis.
🚇

Getting around

Seoul's subway is fast, cheap and English-signed — tap in/out with T-money.

  • Taxis are cheap — hail one or use Kakao T; pay by card or T-money.
  • Jeju has barely any public transport — that's why we're hiring cars (Zhao's on it).

🆘 If something goes wrong

🚓

Emergency numbers

  • 112 — Police
  • 119 — Fire & ambulance
  • 1330 — Korea travel hotline (24/7, English)
  • 120 — Seoul city help line (Dasan)
🛂

Entry & admin

  • Australian passports: K-ETA waived through 31 Dec 2026 — just bring your passport + a return/onward ticket.
  • Korea has an online e-Arrival Card you can fill before you fly — saves the paper form.
  • Passport valid well past the trip; keep a photo/scan saved offline.

🧳 Good to know

🧥

Late-Oct weather

Crisp autumn, peak foliage — roughly 8–18°C. Layers, a warm jacket for evenings and Hallasan, comfy walking shoes. Jeju a touch milder and windier.

🔌

Plugs & power

Type C / F sockets, 220V 60Hz — the round two-pin like most of Europe. Neither Australian nor UK plugs fit, so bring a universal travel adapter each.

🙇

Etiquette

  • No tipping — genuinely not expected anywhere.
  • Shoes off in homes, hanok stays and some restaurants.
  • Two hands when pouring/receiving drinks — pour for others, not yourself.

🗣️ A few phrases

🙂

The basics

  • 안녕하세요 — annyeong-haseyo · hello
  • 감사합니다 — gamsa-hamnida · thank you
  • 네 / 아니요 — ne / aniyo · yes / no
  • 죄송합니다 — joesong-hamnida · sorry / excuse me
🍻

Out & about

  • 얼마예요? — eolma-yeyo · how much?
  • 화장실 어디예요? — hwajangsil eodi-yeyo · where's the toilet?
  • 건배! — geonbae · cheers!
  • 한 잔 더! — han jan deo · one more drink!

🇨🇳 Shanghai extension · Jason + Zhao

🛂

Entry

Australian ordinary-passport holders are currently visa-free for tourism visits of up to 30 days through 31 Dec 2026.

  • Verify Zhao’s passport eligibility separately.
  • Keep onward tickets and the Shanghai hotel address offline.
  • The project has no China Country Pack yet—creating it is an open Crew Desk action.
📲

Pay & navigate

Add a foreign card to Alipay or WeChat Pay before arrival and keep a physical-card / cash backup.

  • Use Didi for taxis and AMap or Apple Maps for navigation.
  • Shanghai Metro accepts major international cards; the base fare starts at CNY 3.
  • Use roaming / an eSIM that works in mainland China and save core bookings offline.
🆘

Shanghai practicals

Early November is mild-to-cool: plan for roughly 10–18°C, with sunset close to 17:05.

  • 110 police · 119 fire · 120 medical.
  • Recommended base: East Nanjing Road / People’s Square for Bund access and Line 2 to PVG.
  • Hotel must offer twin beds and Monday luggage hold.
